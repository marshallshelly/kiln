Kiln mark

Nested arches resolving to a solid core. The engine's layers, and a kiln seen end‑on.

Optical sizing

Three rings hold down to 24 px. Below that the gaps close no matter how the geometry is tuned, because 1 px is 1 px — so the mark has a second cut with two rings and a fatter stroke.

Swap at 24 px. Favicon, tray icon, and nav-bar marks use kiln-mark-small.svg. Everything else uses kiln-mark.svg.

Lockups

Mark and wordmark, at header and nav scale. Clear space on all sides equals one ring stroke.

Colour

The body is currentColor, so one file serves light chrome, dark chrome, and monochrome print. Only the core is fixed.

Amber#F5A93C · the core, never anything else
Ink#0B1226 · body on light
Cream#F4E7D3 · body on dark
Mint#7FC3AC · accents, never the mark

Rules

  • Never recolour the core. The amber is the only fixed value in the system.
  • Never add a third ring below 24 px. That is what the small cut exists for.
  • Never round the leg ends. Butt caps sit the arch flat on its baseline; round caps make it float.
  • Never add a glow, gradient, or shadow. The mark is flat; the atmosphere lives in the page behind it.
  • Never stretch. The 1.24:1 stance is what separates an arch from a rainbow.
